The need to reduce the carbon emission and attain carbon neutrality has resulted in an increase in the need for an alternative source of energy with lower carbon emissions. Photovoltaic is more competitive compared to other renewable energy sources. It also has the potential to meet the energy needs of urban and rural settlements.
